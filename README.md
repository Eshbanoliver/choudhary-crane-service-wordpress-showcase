# Choudhary Crane Service - Crane Rental & Towing Website



Custom WordPress website developed for Choudhary Crane Service, a reliable provider of crane rentals and 24/7 towing solutions in Jaipur, Rajasthan (2025 client project).

## Project Overview
- **Client Industry**: Heavy Equipment Rental & Towing (Construction, Industrial Lifting, Roadside Assistance)
- **My Role**: Full custom WordPress development, theme customization, service layouts, performance optimization
- **Timeline**: 4-6 weeks
- **Team Size**: Solo developer
- **Live Website**: [https://choudharycraneservice.com/](https://choudharycraneservice.com/)  
  *(Confidential client project – visuals & case study only; full demo/login available on request after NDA)*

## Key Features Implemented
- Hero section with strong taglines ("24/7 Crane & Towing Solutions", "From Roadside Rescues to Industrial Lifts — We’ve Got You Covered", "Your Lift. Our Strength")
- Services showcase: Hydra Cranes On Hire, 24 Hours Cranes On Rent, Crane Service Providers (various load capacities/heights), Car Towing Services (short/long distance, 24/7)
- About section emphasizing 16+ years experience, expert operators, precision, trust, and 24/7 availability
- Stats counters: 16+ Years Experience, 30+ Cranes & Equipment, 95% On-Time Job Completion, 5,000+ Happy Customers
- Testimonials carousel with real client feedback on responsiveness, skilled operators, pricing, and efficiency
- Responsive design optimized for heavy equipment visuals and urgent service needs (mobile-first)
- SEO-friendly for local Jaipur crane rental/towing searches

## Technical Stack
- WordPress v6.7+
- PHP 8.2+
- Custom theme + Elementor (or similar builder) for sections, cards, and counters
- Custom CSS for industrial styling, hover effects, and service grids
- JavaScript for testimonials slider, stats animations, smooth interactions
- Plugins: Elementor, contact forms (WPForms or similar), Yoast SEO, image optimization/cache
- Performance: WebP images, lazy loading for crane/equipment photos

## Challenges & Solutions
- Challenge: Conveying reliability for 24/7 heavy lifting/towing → Solution: Prominent stats, testimonials, and "24/7 Availability" highlights
- Challenge: Clearly presenting diverse crane/towing services → Solution: Card layouts with descriptions and icons
- Challenge: Mobile access for emergency roadside needs → Solution: Fully responsive with quick contact CTAs


## What I Learned
- Customizing WordPress for industrial/heavy equipment service sites with trust focus
- Using stats and testimonials to build credibility in 24/7 emergency services
- Optimizing for local searches (Jaipur crane rental, hydra crane hire, car towing)
